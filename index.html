Frequently Asked Questions
How do I schedule a pickup? ›
Tap "Schedule Pickup" on the home screen or bottom nav. Choose your waste types, pick a date and time, and confirm. An EMB-accredited collector will be matched to your request.
Is the service free? ›
Basic pickup scheduling is always free. The collector sets their own service rate. E-Basuramo charges a 10% platform fee per completed pickup. Add-on services (data wipe, RA 9003 certificate) are paid separately.
What does EMB Accredited mean? ›
All collectors hold valid accreditation from the Environmental Management Bureau (EMB) under RA 9003 and RA 6969. This ensures proper and legal disposal of your e-waste.
How do I get my RA 9003 certificate? ›
Add the RA 9003 Certificate add-on (+₱199) when scheduling. After pickup, your certificate is generated automatically and available in Pickup History. SME Pro and Enterprise plans include this automatically.
What is the Secure Data Wipe service? ›
Our certified data destruction service permanently erases all data using DoD 5220.22-M standard. A signed Destruction Certificate is issued, suitable for RA 10173 compliance. Add it for ₱299 per session.
What if my collector doesn't arrive? ›
Tap "Report an Issue" in the Profile menu or contact support via chat. We will rebook your pickup at no extra cost within 24 hours.
How do I cancel or reschedule a pickup? ›
Go to Pickup History, tap the pending pickup, and choose cancel or reschedule from the detail view — up to 2 hours before the window.
